01 Who we are

Patient capital focused on long-term value creation.

Capital deployment is a disciplined craft. SpecialSource was founded to bring institutional expertise to growth-stage investments across high-potential sectors.

We partner with exceptional management teams to build world-class companies. Rather than optimizing for quarterly returns, we invest with a five-to-seven-year horizon, allowing our portfolio companies to scale sustainably and achieve market leadership.

Our check sizes range from $50M to $500M. We add operational rigor, strategic guidance, and our network to every investment.

  • Partner We invest alongside founder conviction.
  • Advise Strategic guidance with operational rigor.
  • Scale Patient capital for sustainable growth.

01

Enterprise Software

B2B SaaS · Infrastructure · Developer Tools

We invest in B2B SaaS companies transforming enterprise operations. Our focus is on founder-led teams building durable competitive advantages through proprietary technology, strong unit economics, and expanding TAMs.

  • Enterprise infrastructure
  • Workflow automation
  • Developer tool ecosystems
02

Healthcare Technology

Digital Health · MedTech · Life Sciences

We partner with innovators reshaping healthcare delivery and outcomes. From diagnostic tools to clinical workflows, we back companies improving access and quality at scale. Our team brings medical device, pharmaceutical, and healthcare IT expertise.

  • Digital therapeutics
  • Clinical workflow software
  • Diagnostic platforms
  • Healthcare operations automation
03

Financial Services

FinTech · Payments · Alternative Credit

We invest in companies modernizing financial infrastructure and expanding access to capital. Our focus ranges from embedded finance platforms to alternative lending and digital banking, always with emphasis on compliance and durable competitive advantage.

  • Embedded finance platforms
  • Payment infrastructure
  • Alternative credit scoring
  • Insurance technology
04

Climate & Sustainability

ClimateeTech · Energy · ESG Infrastructure

We invest in companies solving climate challenges and building a sustainable economy. From renewable energy platforms to carbon management software, our thesis is that climate solutions are the growth sectors of the next decade.

  • Supply chain optimization

Roll-up Strategies · Add-ons · Consolidation
