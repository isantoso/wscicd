01 Who we are

A fifty-year horizon, applied to assets that will outlive us.

Infrastructure is a patience business. Halvorsen Atlantic was founded in a Rotterdam drydock office by a naval architect who refused to design anything that could not be maintained for a century.

Three generations later that constraint is still the firm's operating principle. We take positions in assets we intend to hold — interconnectors, terminals, desalination trains, rail corridors — and we underwrite them with our own engineering, not someone else's forecast.

The group is privately held. There is no quarterly pressure to build something fragile.

  • Own We hold, we don't flip.
  • Build In-house engineering on every mandate.
  • Run Operations teams stay for the asset's life.

02 Capabilities

Five practices,
one balance sheet.

Each practice carries its own engineering bench, its own operators and its own P&L — and draws on the group's capital when the asset deserves it.

01

Grid & Transmission

HVDC · Substations · Interconnection

We develop and operate high-voltage links between markets that cannot otherwise trade power — subsea HVDC, converter stations, and the onshore reinforcement that makes them useful. 6.2 GW of capacity in service, a further 3.1 GW consented.

  • Subsea & underground HVDC
  • 400kV substation delivery
  • Grid-scale storage siting
  • Interconnector regulatory structuring
02

Ports & Freight Terminals

Container · Bulk · Ro-Ro

Quay to gate. We design terminal layouts, build the civil works, install the automation and then run the thing — nineteen terminals across the North Sea, the Gulf and the Strait of Malacca.

  • Automated stacking yards
  • Deepwater quay construction
  • Shore power retrofit
  • Terminal operating concessions
03

Water & Desalination

Reverse osmosis · Reuse · Networks

Potable water at municipal scale, in places where the cheap options ran out a decade ago. 2.4 million cubic metres per day of installed capacity, increasingly coupled directly to renewable generation.

  • Large-train reverse osmosis
  • Industrial water reuse
  • Brine management
  • Network leakage reduction
04

Subsea Engineering

Survey · Lay · Inspection

A fleet of four cable-lay and support vessels, and the survey practice that tells them where to go. We hold the route from geophysical survey through burial, protection and thirty-year inspection contracts.

  • Geophysical & geotechnical survey
  • Cable & pipeline installation
  • Trenching and rock placement
  • ROV inspection & repair
05

Capital & Asset Management

Co-investment · Concessions · Debt

We co-invest alongside pension and sovereign capital on assets our own engineers have diligenced. €14.2 billion under management across four vehicles, with the group always holding a principal stake.

  • Greenfield development equity
  • Availability-based concessions
  • Project debt arrangement
  • Operational asset management
